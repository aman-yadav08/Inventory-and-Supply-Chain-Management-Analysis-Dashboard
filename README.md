📦 Inventory and Supply Chain Management Analysis Dashboard

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze key aspects of inventory management and supply chain performance. The dashboard helps visualize operational metrics such as warehouse utilization, transportation costs, sales trends, lead time, and order status across different regions and product categories.

📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides insights into how inventory and logistics operations perform across different business dimensions. It helps identify inefficiencies, optimize inventory levels, and improve supply chain decision-making.

Key sections of the dashboard include:

Warehouse Utilisation 🏭
Shows how efficiently warehouse capacity is being used. In this analysis, warehouse utilization is 34.08%, indicating available storage capacity and potential for optimization.

Days Sales of Inventory 📦
Measures how quickly inventory is sold and replenished. The dashboard shows an average of 15.56 days, helping evaluate inventory turnover efficiency.

Sales Trend Analysis 📈
Displays yearly sales performance. Units sold show strong growth after 2021, indicating increased product demand.

Transportation Cost Analysis 🚚
Compares transportation costs across regions and product categories, helping identify cost-heavy logistics areas.

Lead Time Analysis ⏱
Analyzes the average lead time by product category, which impacts supply chain responsiveness and delivery performance.

Inventory Distribution 🌍
Visualizes how inventory levels vary across regions and product categories, helping detect overstocking or stock shortages.

Order Status Analysis 📋
Tracks fulfilled, pending, and cancelled orders to provide insights into order management efficiency.

🛠 Tools and Technologies

Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation

Data Analysis – Inventory and supply chain metrics analysis

Interactive Filters – Region and category-based filtering

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how data visualization and analytics can improve supply chain visibility, enabling businesses to:

Monitor inventory performance

Identify logistics cost patterns

Improve warehouse utilization

Support data-driven operational decisions
